Chapter 1: Fractions

Fractions represent parts of a whole. The numerator is the top number and the denominator is the bottom number. A denominator cannot be zero.

Formula: a/b + c/d = (ad + bc)/bd

Theorem: Fractions with the same denominator can be added by adding their numerators and keeping the denominator unchanged.

Chapter 2: Linear Equations

A linear equation has a variable whose highest power is one. Solving an equation means finding values that make both sides equal.

Formula: ax + b = c, so x = (c - b)/a when a is not zero.

Theorem: Adding or subtracting the same value on both sides of an equation preserves its solutions.
