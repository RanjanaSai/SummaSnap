# AI-Powered Chapter Study Notes

Turn one long, multi-chapter study document into organized notes that help a student review each chapter.

## The Unique Kernel

The app summarizes chapter by chapter, highlighting the main points, formulas, and theorems—not just producing one summary for the whole document.

## Who It's For

Students studying from long documents with multiple chapters. They want to review the material without losing the important concepts, formulas, or theorems in a broad summary.

## The Core Loop

A student selects a document from their local machine, asks the app to process it, and reviews the generated notes organized by chapter.

## Inspiration & Identity

Not established yet. The visual style and tone can be explored during PRD planning.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

The learner wants to build an AI-powered notes app and get better at working with an AI coding agent. A more personal motivation for this idea has not been shared.

## What "Working" Looks Like

In a short demo, a student selects a multi-chapter study document and gets chapter-by-chapter notes that make the main points, formulas, and theorems easy to find. The key moment is seeing a long document become structured study material rather than one undifferentiated summary.

## The POC Boundary

Build the document-to-study-notes flow for one locally selected, multi-chapter document. Focus on generating chapter summaries with main points, formulas, and theorems.

## Later

- Create notes and reports from scratch for students or professionals.
- Support cloud-based document browsing.
- Explore professionals as another primary audience.

## Explicitly Cut

- Cloud file browsing in the first proof of concept; selecting a local file is enough to demonstrate the core idea.
- Creating full reports from scratch in the first proof of concept; that is a separate workflow from chapter-by-chapter study summaries.
